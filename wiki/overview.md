# Research Wiki Overview

Welcome to the **Research and Knowledge Base** wiki vault.

## Purpose & Scope

This knowledge base serves as a compounding repository for research papers, domain concepts, empirical datasets, and theoretical frameworks. It bridges primary sources (`.raw/`) with synthesized models (`wiki/`).

## Navigation & Structure

- **`wiki/sources/` & `wiki/papers/`**: Source summaries and academic literature extractions.
- **`wiki/concepts/`**: Core models, terminology, algorithms, and hypotheses.
- **`wiki/entities/`**: Protocols, tools, organizations, and systems.
- **`wiki/thesis/`**: Evolving synthesis on the state of the field.
- **`wiki/gaps/`**: Identified open questions, contradictions, and areas needing research.
- **`wiki/comparisons/`**: Systematic side-by-side analysis.
- **`wiki/questions/`**: Answers to research queries filed for future recall.
- **`wiki/meta/`**: Dashboards, health checks, and conventions.
